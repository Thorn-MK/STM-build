# STM-build
Attempt to design and build a scanning tunneling microscope ( STM ).

Project Status Update :

This project was initiated to explore nanotechnology through the design of a Scanning Tunneling Microscope. During the research phase, I learned about tunneling physics, atomic-scale resolution, and the engineering challenges involved in STM systems.

Through this process, I realized that while I am strongly interested in nanotechnology, microscopy-focused instrumentation is not an area I find engaging. I am more interested in applied nanoscale systems, fabrication, and integration with larger robotic or electronic platforms.

For this reason, I decided not to continue with a physical STM build and instead redirect my efforts toward projects that better align with my interests, such as robotics and autonomous systems.

This project remains as a record of my research and design exploration.
